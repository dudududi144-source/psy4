  3605→T1.4 — Implemented the analysis in `referenceListenerV2.ts::extractFeaturesFromBuffer()`:
  3606→- Spectral crest: peak magnitude / mean magnitude. Single pass over avgMag.
  3607→- Spectral slope (dB/oct): linear regression on ln(freq) vs ln(mag), converted via `b * ln(2) * 20 / ln(10)`. Bins below 80 Hz skipped (DC/sub interference). Clamped to -36..+6.
  3608→- Fundamental frequency (f0): Harmonic Product Spectrum — multiply downsampled spectra (depth 4) over 80-2000 Hz, pick the bin with the largest product. Used by HNR + inharmonicity.
  3609→- HNR (0..1): sum energy in ±2-bin windows around the first 10 harmonic bins (f0, 2·f0, ..., 10·f0) divided by total spectral energy above threshold.
  3610→- Inharmonicity (0..1): find spectral peaks (local maxima ≥ 3× mean); for each peak above f0, compute relative deviation from the nearest integer harmonic; mean deviation × 5 (so 20% deviation → 1.0).
  3611→- Transient sharpness (0..1): for each detected transient, measure attack rise time (10%→90% of peak); sharpness = 1 - rise/30ms. Capped look-back at 30ms.
  3612→- Transient decay (ms): for each detected transient, find time to drop to 10% of peak; averaged. Capped look-ahead at 300ms.
  3613→- Stereo balance (-1..1): (R_energy - L_energy) / (L+R), reusing the lEnergy/rEnergy already computed for stereoWidth.
  3614→- Stereo correlation (-1..1): the SIGNED L·R correlation (was previously only used via Math.abs for stereoWidth). Now stored as its own field.
  3615→- M/S ratio (0..1): side energy / (mid + side) energy, computed via a strided loop (caps analysis samples at 50000 for efficiency).
  3616→- Added module-level `clampT1(v, lo, hi)` helper that guards against NaN/Infinity (the existing inline `Math.max(0, Math.min(1, ...))` patterns don't guard NaN).
